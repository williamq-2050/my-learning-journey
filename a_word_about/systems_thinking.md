# Systems Thinking

Systems Thinking is an approach to understanding organizations and complex problems by looking at the whole system and the interactions among its parts, rather than optimizing each component independently.

## Dr. Russell L. Ackoff (1919–2009)
Dr. Russell L. Ackoff was a major contributor to systems thinking and organizational theory. He emphasized understanding the purpose of the whole system first, and then determining how its components should work together to achieve that purpose.

## Dr. W. Edwards Deming (1900–1993)
Dr. W. Edwards Deming applied systems thinking particularly to management, quality, and continuous improvement. He strongly argued against managing organizations primarily through individual targets, quotas, rankings, and local performance metrics when those measures encourage people or departments to optimize themselves at the expense of the organization.
